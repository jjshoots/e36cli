# Live RAM probe — ECU 1429373 (Euro MS41.0), 2026-10-25

Read-only probe (cmds 0x00/0x06 only) of the owner's running car at warm idle
(coolant 84→90 °C, ~700 rpm), to recover the variant-specific addresses missing
from the logger XML (no 1429373 column). Raw data: `data/1429373-ram-sweeps.json`
(3 full sweeps of 4 windows) and `data/1429373-validate-rounds.json` (20-round
re-read, 8 clean rounds before the engine was shut off). A representative wire
capture is `car/captures/car-ram-dump.jsonl`.

## Method

1. Three timed sweeps of windows 0xDA2A/0xE980 (anchors), 0xEA00–0xF1FF (trim
   hunt), 0xF880–0xFD3F (load/battery hunt), in 0x60-byte chunks, short burst
   sessions (flaky USB, see below).
2. Anchor check: verified params (rpm/coolant/iat/vss) decoded correctly from
   the dumps → dump integrity confirmed. RPM is u16 **little-endian** here.
3. Hypothesis test: 1429861 (sibling Euro MS41.0) addresses applied directly.
4. Signature scan: change-over-time + value-range fingerprints for battery etc.
5. 20× re-read validation of all candidates (~1 s apart).

## Verdicts

| Param | Address | Confidence | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Injector PW | 0xECBC u16le ×0.00534 ms | HIGH (addr+scale) | 2.67–2.78 ms at warm idle, all rounds; = 1429861 addr |
| STFT B1 | 0xED5C u16le, (x−32768)×100/65535 % | HIGH | frozen at EXACTLY 0.00% — bank 1 open loop (dead B1 sensor) |
| STFT B2 | 0xED96 same | HIGH | actively regulating −2.25…−5.9%, every sample distinct |
| LTFT B1/B2 | 0xED6E / 0xEDA8 u16le | HIGH addr, LOW scaling | b1 static 4321, b2 adapting 4394–4525; documented (x−32768) scaling gives −43% = wrong; x/4096-as-factor hypothesis → +5.5% / +7–10% |
| Additive B1/B2 | 0xED66 / 0xEDA0 u16le | MED addr, LOW scaling | b1 frozen 0 (open loop), b2 live 66–75; documented 0x8000 midpoint wrong; unsigned ×0.00534 ms hypothesis (~0.36 ms) |
| Lambda status B1/B2 | 0xED58 / 0xED92 u8 | MED | constant 0x02 (B1) / 0x01 (B2); hypothesis bit0 = closed-loop active (doc's bit3 does not match) |
| Battery | **0xFB45** u8 ×0.10196 V | HIGH (**verified**) | 13.97–14.38 V with alternator ripple, 15 samples running; = 1429861 addr − 2; 0xFAB5 tracks identically (alternate/filtered copy) |
| Engine load | 0xFAFC u16le | MED addr, LOW scaling | raw 51–54 stable at idle (= 1429861 addr); ×0.021195 → 1.1 units, unit interpretation uncertain |
| Runtime cut | — | NOT FOUND | 0xFAE6 (1429861) is a fast-changing live byte here |
| Launch speed, O2 heater duties | — | NOT FOUND | candidates static/implausible |

**Bottom line: 1429373 shares 1429861's trim-block layout exactly; the
load/battery region is shifted/different (battery −2); several documented
*scalings* are wrong for this program.** The bank-asymmetry fingerprint
(B1 frozen everywhere lambda-related, B2 live) matches the car's known dead
bank-1 O2 sensor and is strong evidence the addresses are right, not
coincidental.

## Owner-relevant finding: lambda adaptation status

- Bank 2: closed-loop fueling ACTIVE (STFT moving every second, LTFT slowly
  adapting, additive non-zero). The DME is using lambda adaptations on bank 2.
- Bank 1: lambda control FROZEN (STFT pinned at 0.00%, LTFT/additive static,
  status byte differs from B2) — consistent with the flat-lined 4.89 V B1
  sensor and fault code 25 (B1 heater open circuit). Bank 1 runs on canned
  maps + bank-2-learned corrections only.

## Wire-stability notes (matter for future probes)

- 0x06 reads of 0xF8 bytes exceed the default 0.3 s deadline (response alone
  ≈ 290 ms at 872 B/s) — use Timing(deadline=1.0) and ≤0x60 chunks.
- The FTDI adapter dropped off the USB bus repeatedly under sustained traffic
  tonight (re-enumerated as ttyUSB1 once while a stale fd held ttyUSB0). Burst
  sessions (≤6 chunks), closing the port between bursts, completed 114/114
  chunks without a single wire failure. Suspect the cable, not the car.
- BUG found for the repo: `SerialTransport.flush_input()` lets `termios.error`
  escape unwrapped (it is not an OSError) when the device vanishes mid-session.
- The 0xF880+ region contains calibration-table ramps (monotone byte runs) and
  an apparent 0x100-periodic partial mirror — signature scans must exclude
  sequential-neighbor contexts or they match table axes.
